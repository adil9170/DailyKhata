      Write how you write.

      Hindi, English, or both in one line. 1,500 or 1.5k or 1500/- — all fine.

      Also in there: a login for your helper that can't see your profit, more than one shop, Excel for your CA, a photo of a supplier's bill, and a backup every night.

      Questions

      Do I have to type in English?

      No. Write the way you write in your register — “chai 40”, “bijli bill 1450”, “चाय ४०”, “Verma ne 500 diye”.

      What if the internet goes?

      Keep working. Everything is saved on your phone first and goes up by itself when the network is back.

      Can my helper see my profit?

      Only if you let them. Everyone gets their own login and you choose what they can see and do.

      Is it an app?

      It opens in your browser, on any phone or computer. Tap “Add to Home screen” and it sits there like an app, with your shop's logo.

      Who can see my numbers?

      Only the people you add to your shop. Each shop is kept separate, and a copy is saved every night.
