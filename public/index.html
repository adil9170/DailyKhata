A day at the counter

From shutter up to closing time.

  1. 7:30 am

    Shutter up

    Count the notes in the drawer. That's your opening cash, and the day starts there.

  2. 11:00 am

    Stock arrives, chai is ordered

    “Sting 2 box 1020, chai 40” — type it or say it. It's in the khata before the delivery boy has left.

  3. 4:15 pm

    “Kal de dunga”

    Make a bill, mark it udhaar, send it to the customer's WhatsApp. It stays on their name until they pay.

  4. 9:30 pm

    Closing

    Sales, spending and what's left — on one screen. Count the drawer again and you'll know if anything is short.

  5. Sunday

    The week, at a glance

    How much came in, where it went, and what you made — ready to send to your CA as Excel or PDF.

Questions

Shopkeepers ask.

Do I have to type in English?

No. Write the way you write in your register — “chai 40”, “bijli bill 1450”, “चाय ४०”, “Verma ne 500 diye”. Numbers can be 1,500, 1.5k or 1500/-.

What if the internet goes?

Keep working. Everything is saved on the phone first and goes up quietly when the connection is back.

Can my staff see my profit?

Only if you let them. Each person gets their own login and you choose what they can see and do.

Is it a phone app?

It opens in the browser on any phone or computer. Tap “Add to Home screen” and it sits there like any other app, with your shop's logo.

Who can see my numbers?

Only the people you add to your shop. Each shop's data is kept separate, and a copy is saved every night.

Closing time

The register stays at the counter. The khata goes home with you.
