Questions shopkeepers ask

Do I have to type in English?

No. Write the way you write in your register — “chai 40”, “bijli bill 1450”, “चाय ४०”, “Verma ne 500 diye”. Numbers can be 1,500, 1.5k or 1500/-.

What if the internet goes?

Keep working. Everything is saved on the phone first and goes up quietly when the connection is back.

Can my staff see my profit?

Only if you let them. Each person gets their own login and you choose what they can see and do.

Is it a phone app?

It opens in the browser on any phone or computer. Tap “Add to Home screen” and it sits there like any other app, with your shop's logo.

Who can see my numbers?

Only the people you add to your shop. Each shop's data is kept separate.
