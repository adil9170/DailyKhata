    Questions

    Do I have to type in English?

    No. “chai 40”, “bijli bill 1450”, “चाय ४०”, “Verma ne 500 diye” all work.

    What if the internet goes?

    Keep working. It goes up by itself when the network is back.

    Who can see my numbers?

    Only the people you add to your shop. Each shop is kept separate, with a copy saved every night.
